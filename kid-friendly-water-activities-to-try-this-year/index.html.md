# 7 Fun Water Activities for Kids to Enjoy Family Summer Fun

Kid-friendly water activities are a summer staple at my house. Kids love water, so finding engaging **water activities for kids** is a great way to keep your little ones entertained and out of trouble while school is out. Whether you are looking for energetic **youth water activities** or memorable **family water activities**, having a go-to list makes all the difference when temperatures rise. Next time your kids start bickering or saying they are super bored, roll out one of these simple **water activities kids** will adore, and the fun won’t ever stop. Go ahead and try them all—you’ll have just as much fun as the kids.

| Activity | Best Age Group | Prep Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Boating & River Wading | All Ages / Families | 10–15 mins |
| Backyard Bath & Sprinklers | Toddlers & Young Kids | 5 mins |
| Water Balloons & Ice Dig | School-Age & Youth | 15–30 mins |

## 1. Boating

One of the best kid-friendly water activities is boating. I’ve taken my kids out in a boat plenty of times and they always have mega fun. According to official safety guidelines from the [U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary](https://www.uscgboating.org), safety comes first: make sure every child wears a properly fitted life jacket just in case things get a little crazy and someone goes overboard. You can make your way around the lake while you eat snacks, tell funny stories, and make memories no one will forget. For more seasonal inspiration, explore our complete [parenting resource hub](https://parenting.allwomenstalk.com/).

## 2. River Wading

There’s nothing my kids love more than wading in the river. Luckily, we only have to walk to the end of our block to find one. The water is only a couple of inches deep so they can go right in and find rocks or other treasures. Organizations like the [National Wildlife Federation](https://www.nwf.org) encourage this kind of hands-on nature exploration for children. I made them collectors by pulling tulle in a quilting hoop so they can slide it under the water and see what they can find. Just be sure to have your kids wear shoes so they don’t get hurt if they step on something sharp, much like preparing for a [frozen treasure challenge](#6) back in the yard.

## 3. Water Balloons

If you’ve never had a family water balloon fight, you are seriously missing out. No doubt your kids have tossed water balloons at a party, but when you only have a couple of family members, you each get so many more balloons. Fill up a few hundred and let them fly all afternoon. You’ll all have a great time!

Here are a few classic game variations to try with your balloons:

- Water balloon toss with a partner
- Target practice into plastic laundry baskets or buckets
- Water balloon relay races across the yard

## 4. Sprinkler Snake

When I was a kid, running through the sprinkler was such a treat. This is a variation that is sure to produce lots of giggles and hilarious laughter. Set the sprinkler up on the lawn and as your kids run through, pull and twist it from across the yard. They won’t know where it’ll end up next and that’s part of the fun. On exceptionally warm days, checking weather alerts from the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov) ensures everyone stays safe while enjoying outdoor play, especially when paired with simple [backyard bath](#5) setups for younger toddlers.

## 5. Backyard Bath

I would only recommend this idea for very young kids and only if your backyard is totally fenced. Older kids won’t like the embarrassment, I can assure you of that, but babies and toddlers will love it. Fill the kiddie pool with warm water, bubble bath and toys and clean your little one up out in the open.

## 6. Frozen Treasure

This is a fun and entertaining activity that will keep kids busy for quite a while. First, fill a small plastic tub with water, then toss in a bunch of small toys, coins and other goodies. Freeze the entire thing. Give your child some dull utensils that they can use to chip away at the ice and reveal the treasures hidden within. It makes a fantastic, focused cooling activity after an energetic [water balloon battle](#3).

## 7. Water Piñata

Kids love opening a piñata and getting all the candy, so this is sure to be fun for them too. Fill a large, durable plastic bag with water and tie it shut tightly. Secure it to a tree limb. Let your kids whack it with a stick until it breaks open and showers them all with water. What a great way to cool off on a hot summer day!

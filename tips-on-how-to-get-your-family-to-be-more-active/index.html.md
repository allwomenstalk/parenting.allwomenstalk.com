# 7 Practical Ways to Get Your Family to Be More Active

Are you looking for advice on how to get your family to be more active? Many moms are. They want their family to be a healthy one and know that exercise is a big part of that. But it is sometimes hard to get everyone motivated and off of the couch. Following official [CDC physical activity guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/children.html) can help ensure everyone gets enough daily movement. These are some great ideas to help you figure out how to get your family to be more active.

## 1. Be Active Yourself

The very first step in figuring out how to get your family to be more active is to be active yourself. You have to set an example for them. It really is true that children are more likely to mimic what you do versus what you say. Telling them being active is good for them is not going to make as much impact as actually living an active lifestyle before them. When they see that mom makes an effort to exercise daily, they are going to be much more likely to make that part of their own life, even if it isn’t until they are young adults themselves. Incorporating daily movement into your routine naturally encourages kids to join in on [active play](#2) without feeling pressured.

## 2. Invite Active Play

Make being active fun. Spend time with your kids outside and run with them. Play Frisbee or catch. Participate in playing tag or hide and seek with them. Make it a goal to do whatever gets them up and moving around.

Here are a few quick active play ideas to try together:

- Tag, hide-and-seek, and relay races
- Frisbee tossing and backyard catch
- Obstacle courses using simple outdoor props

## 3. Enroll Your Kids in Activities

I am not an advocate for having your children be overscheduled, but I do think that it is a good thing to have them in one or two activities that encourage exercise. There are so many options that kids can choose from to be active now. There is soccer, baseball, basketball, swimming, gymnastics, dance and many others. Surely there is something that every child can enjoy.

To help balance your family's schedule, here is a quick overview of activity types:

| Activity Type | Recommended Frequency | Key Family Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily Outdoor Play | 60+ minutes daily | Boosts energy & mood |
| Evening Family Walks | 3–5 times weekly | Low-impact bonding time |
| Organized Sports / Classes | 1–2 times weekly | Builds teamwork & skills |

Finding the right mix keeps physical activity rewarding rather than overwhelming for your kids.

## 4. Limit Screen Time

One reason that families are not as active as they should be is that they have too much screen time. Our family loves our electronic devices as much as the next family, but the truth is that they do interfere with our ability to be active. Sometimes it is just best to have a limit for screen time, aligned with [American Academy of Pediatrics guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/). This frees your kids up to do so many other things. They are really missing out on a lot of fun things, like just going outside to play, if they are constantly using their electronics.

Consider establishing clear screen boundaries for the household:

- No screens during family mealtimes
- Designated tech-free bedrooms after bedtime
- Active play time before turning on evening entertainment

## 5. Take Walks

A walk is healthy for everyone. So why not enjoy taking a walk as a family? It is the perfect after dinner activity. It doesn’t cost you anything. According to [Mayo Clinic fitness insights](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/fitness/art-20046434), regular daily walks build cardiovascular endurance while creating space for daily conversation. It is a great way to get a little exercise in while getting some time together as a family, too.

## 6. Make Exercise Fun

Here is one of the beautiful things about children. Children do not know any different to what you teach them. If you teach them that exercise is fun, they are going to believe that exercise is fun. If you act like exercise is a chore to be dreaded, that is what they will learn. You have a lot of impact over their thoughts and feelings about being active by how you portray yours. Simple routines like [taking family walks](#5) can turn regular exercise into a joyful family ritual.

## 7. Make Exercise Part of Daily Living

There are parts of our lives that we all know are needful and good for us. We know we need sleep and healthy foods. We also need exercise. If you treat your need for exercise, and your children’s need for exercise, as a need to be healthy, that is what it will be. It won’t be something that you have to think about because it is just understood that it is necessary. Pairing physical exercise with habits like [limiting screen time](#4) ensures your home environment naturally supports wellness.

It is a difficult task to get your family to be active but it can be done. Is your family an active family? How have you accomplished that?
